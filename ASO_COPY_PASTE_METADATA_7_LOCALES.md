# Nafsi ASO — brand + ranking (copy-paste for ASC)

## Is this good for ranking?

**Yes for brand + solid ASO. Not “keyword spam to rank #1 overnight.”**

| Approach | Ranking | Brand |
|---|---|---|
| `Nafsi Muslim Dhikr Quran Fajr Lock` in the name | Slightly more keywords | Looks cheap, Apple may flag, weak brand |
| **Name = Nafsi + nafs promise · Subtitle = muslim/dhikr/blocker** | Strong category keywords | Brand stays premium ← **this** |
| Name only `Nafsi` | Weak discovery | Strong brand, poor ASO |

Name is what people remember. Subtitle + Keywords do the search work. Screenshots, ratings, and retention move ranking more than metadata alone.

**Rules:** Name 30 · Subtitle 30 · Keywords 100 · no spaces after commas in Keywords · don’t repeat Name/Subtitle words in Keywords.

Bundle ID stays `com.scrolldeeds.app`.
