# Privacy Policy for ScrollDeeds

**Last Updated: November 3, 2025**

ScrollDeeds ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application ScrollDeeds (the "App").

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
- **App Selection Data**: We store which apps you choose to lock on your device locally using Apple's Family Controls framework.
- **Onboarding Responses**: Your responses to the initial questionnaire are stored locally on your device.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **Audio Recordings**: When you recite dhikr, we temporarily record your voice using your device's microphone. This audio is processed on-device using Apple's Speech Recognition framework and sent to our secure verification service to verify your recitation.
- **Usage Data**: We track your practice sessions, unlock periods, and app usage statistics locally on your device.

### 1.3 Device Permissions
The App requires the following permissions:
- **Family Controls**: To lock and unlock selected applications
- **Speech Recognition**: To verify your dhikr recitation
- **Microphone**: To record audio for dhikr verification
- **Notifications**: To send reminders about unlock periods and app locking

## 2. How We Use Your Information

### 2.1 Audio Verification
- Audio recordings are sent to our secure webhook service (hosted on n8n.cloud) for verification purposes only.
- Audio data is processed to verify that you have completed the required dhikr recitation.
- **We do not store audio recordings**. Audio data is processed and immediately discarded after verification.
- Audio data is transmitted over HTTPS to ensure secure transmission.

### 2.2 Local Data Storage
- All app selection data, practice history, and user preferences are stored locally on your device using UserDefaults.
- **We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal identifiers** such as your name, email address, or phone number.
- **We do not use analytics or tracking services** that would identify you personally.

### 2.3 App Functionality
- Your selected apps are locked using Apple's ManagedSettings framework, which operates entirely on your device.
- We use Family Controls to manage screen time restrictions as requested by you.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

### 3.1 Local Storage
- All user data (app selections, practice history, preferences) is stored locally on your device.
- This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to external servers.
- You can delete this data at any time by uninstalling the App.

### 3.2 Audio Data Security
- Audio recordings are transmitted securely using HTTPS encryption.
- Audio data is processed by our verification service and immediately discarded.
- We do not maintain databases of audio recordings.

### 3.3 Data Retention
- Local data remains on your device until you delete the App.
- Audio data is not retained after verification is complete.

## 4. Third-Party Services

### 4.1 Webhook Verification Service
- We use a third-party webhook service (n8n.cloud) to verify dhikr recitations.
- Audio data is sent to this service for verification purposes only.
- The service provider is contractually obligated to process data securely and not retain audio recordings.

### 4.2 Apple Services
- We use Apple's Family Controls, ManagedSettings, and Speech Recognition frameworks.
- These services operate according to Apple's privacy policies and terms of service.

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

**We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties**, except:
- When required by law or legal process
- To protect our rights or the safety of users
- With your explicit consent

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

### 6.1 Device Permissions
You can revoke permissions at any time through your device settings:
- **Settings → ScrollDeeds → Permissions**

### 6.2 Data Deletion
- To delete all local data, simply uninstall the App from your device.
- All data stored locally will be removed when you uninstall the App.

### 6.3 Audio Recording
- You can stop recording at any time during dhikr recitation.
- Audio is only recorded when you actively use the dhikr verification feature.

## 7. Children's Privacy

ScrollDeeds is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

## 8. International Users

- By using the App, you consent to the processing of your information as described in this Privacy Policy
