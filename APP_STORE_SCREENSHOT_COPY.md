## 🎨 **DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS**

### **Header Style:**
- **Background:** Dark green (#1a4d3a or similar)
- **Text Color:** White
- **Font:** Bold, 28-32pt
- **Position:** Top of screen

### **Tagline (Pill Button):**
- **Background:** White
- **Text Color:** Dark green
- **Font:** Semi-bold, 16-18pt
- **Shape:** Rounded pill (high corner radius)
- **Position:** Below header or floating

### **Body Copy:**
- **Text Color:** Dark text on light background (or white on dark)
- **Font:** Regular to medium, 14-16pt
- **Line Height:** 1.4-1.6
- **Alignment:** Center or left-aligned
- **Spacing:** Generous padding
